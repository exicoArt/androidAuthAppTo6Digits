The address your device got is a private or local address. A public website should never resolve to one, which is a strong sign of tampering.

What to do now

  • Do not enter your password or any 6-digit code on the site you were heading to.
  • Switch networks, for example from public Wi-Fi to mobile data.
  • Restart your router, and turn on Private DNS in your phone's network settings.
  • Open the service through its official app or by typing its address yourself.

About this page

The app compares the address your device's normal DNS gives for a site with answers from trusted, encrypted DNS. If they differ in a worrying way, it sends you here instead of showing a code. You can see an example warning.

Leave this page
This page has no login form and collects nothing. It cannot check your connection itself: it only displays the values in the link that brought you here, so treat a warning as meaningful only when the app sent you.
